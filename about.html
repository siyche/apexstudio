About

We're a visual storytelling team focused on real estate.

I’m Stephen Chen, a Newcastle, Washington–based photographer specializing in real estate, commercial, and land/landscape photography. As an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, I also bring experience in aerial photography, cinematography, and search-and-rescue support.

Alongside my work as a software engineer, I’ve spent the past several years developing my passion for photography and real estate. I’ve worked with real estate companies, agents, homeowners, and private clients throughout the area, creating thoughtful visuals designed to showcase each property at its best.
